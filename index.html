The Alchemy of Resistance

From Molten Wax to Living Color

Follow the time-honored five-stage transformation from raw natural fiber to finished multi-layered textile artwork.

Stage 01

Motif Drafting

Freehand pencil sketching on unbleached raw silk or organic flax.

Stage 02

Hot Canting Wax

Applying molten beeswax at 75°C with copper spouts to block dye penetration.

Stage 03

Vat Immersion

Immersion in fermented indigo vats; fabric oxidizes from yellow-green to deep blue.

Stage 04

Crackle Veining

Controlled fracturing of wax creates the signature artisanal marbled crackles.

Stage 05

Boil-Out Reveal

Simmering water rinse melts away wax resist, followed by solar drying in courtyard.
