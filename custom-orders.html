How the Bespoke Journey Works

From your initial concept to the final solar-cured piece delivered to your door, every commission moves through a carefully guided atelier process.

01
Begin

Submit Your Brief

Share your desired dimensions, fabric choice, reference photos, or interior color palettes. We use your visual direction to understand the character, scale, mood, and practical needs of your commission before the design process begins.

Concept Intake
02
Refine

Digital Proof & Swatches

Maya translates your direction into a digital motif layout and prepares physical botanical dye test swatches. You can compare pattern scale, tonal movement, fabric behavior, and color before we begin the final hand-dyeing process.

Design Approval
03
Create

Atelier Hand Dyeing

Our master artisans hand-wax, clamp, fold, and submerge your piece into our living fermentation vats. Each layer is carefully developed by hand, allowing natural variations and subtle tonal shifts to become part of the finished textile.

Artisan Production
04
Complete

Finishing & Delivery

Wax is carefully removed, edges are hand-finished, and every textile is inspected for finish and consistency. Your completed heirloom is then wrapped and placed inside an archival cedar box for safe delivery to your door.

Technique Directory

Select Your Resist Craft Technique

Each traditional method produces distinct visual signatures and organic crackle textures.

Indonesian Heritage

Freehand Canting Wax Batik

Molten wax is penned directly onto stretched fabric with copper spouts, allowing custom hand-drawn florals and sacred geometry.

Best For: Art Panels, Wedding Veils, Kimonos
Japanese Tradition

Itajime Clamp Shibori

Fabric is folded accordion-style and clamped firmly between carved cherrywood blocks before immersion into deep indigo vats.

Best For: Cushion Sets, Table Runners, Blankets
Patience & Precision

Production Stages & Lead Times

True slow craft requires natural curing, atmospheric oxidation, careful handwork, and time. Every stage is intentionally paced to protect the character, depth, and individuality of the textile.

01
Week 1 Foundation

Design & Swatch Test

Color balancing, botanical recipe formulation, digital scale layout, and client swatch approval establish the visual and technical direction before the final textile enters production.

Concept & Color Development
02
Weeks 2 – 3 Transformation

Wax Penning & Vat Dips

Multi-layer canting wax resist application is followed by repeated vat immersions and controlled oxidation. Sunlight, air, dye depth, and curing time gradually build the final tonal character of the textile.

Hand Dyeing & Oxidation
03
Week 4 Completion

Boil-Out & Delivery

Hot water wax removal reveals the final pattern, followed by hand-rolled hem tailoring, finishing inspection, framing where required, archival packaging, and worldwide insured shipping.

Final Finish & Dispatch

Typical Lead Time

Approximately 4 weeks from design approval
