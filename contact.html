Answers & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our natural dyes, workshops, and custom orders.

All our handcrafted pieces are permanently fixed using natural mineral mordants. To ensure your piece stays luminous: hand-wash cold with pH-neutral soap, do not bleach, avoid direct scorching sun drying, and iron on medium heat with steam while slightly damp.
True natural botanical dyes undergo a gentle, graceful patina rather than ugly chemical degradation. Indigo and madder root have survived in museum tapestries for thousands of years. We test every recipe for standard lightfastness.
Because our class sizes are strictly capped at 8 participants and dye vats are pre-fermented for each student, cancellations requested at least 7 days before class receive a 100% refund. Rescheduling is free up to 48 hours prior to class.
Standard bespoke apparel and living space textiles typically take 3 to 4 weeks. Large architectural wall tapestries require 4 to 6 weeks.
Yes! We ship worldwide via carbon-neutral tracked DHL Express in cedar-lined archival boxes with our physical certificate of craftsmanship.
In our "Modern Tie-Dye & Ice Dye Lab", you are welcome to bring up to 2 personal garments made of 100% natural fibers (pure cotton, linen, silk, or wool). Synthetic fabrics cannot absorb natural plant dyes.
