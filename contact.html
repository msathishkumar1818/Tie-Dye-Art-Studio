Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding studio visits, care, and commissions.

Hand-wash cold with pH-neutral soap. Dry flat in the shade. Iron medium with steam while slightly damp.
True botanical dyes develop a gentle patina over years. All recipes are tested for standard lightfastness.
100% refund up to 7 days before class. Free rescheduling up to 48 hours prior to your session.
Apparel and home textiles typically take 3 to 4 weeks. Large architectural tapestries take 4 to 6 weeks.
